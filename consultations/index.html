How a consultation works

  1. Book

    Send the form below on WhatsApp or call the clinic. You'll get a confirmed time.

  2. Bring your reports

    All of them, with your medicines. For an online call, share them on WhatsApp first.

  3. Consult

    Your story, an examination and an honest conversation about what is going on.

  4. Your plan

    Medicines, tests and targets in writing, with a date to follow up.

Request an appointment

The clinic will confirm the time with you on WhatsApp or by phone.

Please bring

  • All recent reports and discharge summaries
  • Your medicines, or their strips
  • Home sugar or blood pressure readings, if you take them
  • A list of your questions

Booking questions

How do I book?

Fill in the form on this page and send it on WhatsApp, or call the clinic. The team confirms a time with you.

Are the timings fixed?

Timings can change, so please call or send a WhatsApp message before you visit, especially for the clinic.

Does he consult on Sundays?

No. Consultations are Monday to Saturday. In an emergency, call 112 or 108.

Can a family member come along?

Yes. Many patients find it helpful, and a family member can also join an online call.

How do online consultations work?

Book as usual and choose online. Share your reports on WhatsApp beforehand; the clinic sends you the call details.

What if I need to cancel?

Let the clinic know as early as you can on WhatsApp or by phone, so the slot can go to someone else.

Not feeling right? Start with a proper conversation.

Book a visit at the clinic or at East Delhi Medical Centre, or consult online. Bring your reports and medicines — Dr Gangwal will go through them with you, in English or Hindi.
