Diabetes and metabolic health

Diabetes rarely travels alone. Blood pressure, cholesterol, the kidneys and the eyes are looked after together, with targets you can track at home.

Type 2 diabetes

Starting treatment, adjusting it, and keeping complications away.

Often for
A raised HbA1c or sugar reading, thirst, tiredness, weight loss.
What to expect
HbA1c and kidney tests, a medicine plan, home-testing targets.

Prediabetes

Catching sugar early, while lifestyle changes work best.

Often for
HbA1c 5.7 to 6.4%, or fasting sugar 100 to 125 mg/dL.
What to expect
A practical plan for food, activity and weight, and a yearly check.

High blood pressure

Confirming the diagnosis properly and finding the simplest treatment that works.

Often for
Raised readings at home or at a pharmacy, headaches, diabetes.
What to expect
Home readings, a few tests, then a treatment plan.

Cholesterol

Deciding when cholesterol needs medicine, based on your overall risk.

Often for
A high LDL on a check-up, diabetes, family history.
What to expect
Risk assessment and targets; statins explained, side effects included.

Fevers and infections

Most fevers settle. Some need tests, close watching and, at times, a hospital bed. Knowing which is which is the job.

Viral fever

Care for the usual fevers, and spotting the ones that are not usual.

Often for
Fever with body ache, cold or sore throat.
What to expect
Examination, tests only when needed, clear warning signs to watch.

Dengue

Watching platelets, fluids and warning signs through the critical days.

Often for
Fever in the monsoon and after, with body ache or rash.
What to expect
Blood counts at the right times, fluids, and admission if needed.

Malaria and typhoid

Confirming the cause before treatment, then the right medicines.

Often for
High or recurring fever, chills, stomach upset.
What to expect
Blood tests, a full course of treatment, a follow-up check.

Urinary tract infections

Treating infection and finding out why it keeps coming back.

Often for
Burning, frequency, fever, back pain.
What to expect
Urine tests and culture, the right antibiotic.

Acute diarrhoea and stomach infections

Fluids, treatment and watching for dehydration.

Often for
Loose motions, vomiting, cramps.
What to expect
Rehydration advice, tests if it persists.

Cough and chest infections

Telling a simple cough from pneumonia or something else.

Often for
A cough that lingers, fever, breathlessness.
What to expect
Examination, a chest X-ray when needed, treatment.

Everyday internal medicine

Internal medicine is care for adults when the cause is not yet clear, or when more than one problem is in play.

Liver problems and jaundice

Making sense of abnormal liver tests, fatty liver and jaundice.

Often for
Yellow eyes, raised liver enzymes on a report, alcohol use.
What to expect
Blood tests and ultrasound, then a plan.

Abdominal pain and acidity

Finding the cause of pain, acidity and indigestion.

Often for
Recurring pain, bloating, heartburn.
What to expect
Examination, targeted tests, treatment.

Headache and migraine

Treating headache and ruling out the causes that need attention.

Often for
Frequent headaches, migraine attacks.
What to expect
History and examination; tests only when the story points to them.

Joint and muscle pain

Working out where pain comes from and easing it safely.

Often for
Aches, stiffness, swollen joints.
What to expect
Examination, blood tests when needed, a treatment plan.

Sleep problems

Looking at the reasons behind poor sleep before reaching for pills.

Often for
Trouble falling or staying asleep, daytime tiredness.
What to expect
A review of habits, medicines and health problems.

Adult vaccination

Vaccines adults often miss.

Often for
Flu season, travel, diabetes, age over 60.
What to expect
Advice on which vaccines suit you.

Critical care

Some illnesses need a hospital bed, close monitoring and quick decisions. Dr Gangwal works in internal medicine and critical care, so he knows that path from the inside.

Severe infections

Infections that spread or affect the blood pressure, kidneys or breathing, including severe dengue.

Often for
High fever with drowsiness, low blood pressure, very low urine.
What to expect
Admission, close monitoring and treatment in hospital.

Acute pancreatitis

Inflammation of the pancreas that needs hospital care.

Often for
Severe upper abdominal pain with vomiting.
What to expect
Admission, fluids, pain relief and tests to find the cause.

Diabetic emergencies

Very high or very low sugar that needs urgent treatment.

Often for
Confusion, vomiting, deep breathing, or sugar that won't come down.
What to expect
Treatment in hospital, then a safer plan for home.

Breathing difficulty and pneumonia

When an infection or another illness makes breathing hard.

Often for
Breathlessness at rest, fast breathing, low oxygen.
What to expect
Oxygen, tests and treatment in hospital.

After a hospital stay

Bringing medicines, tests and recovery together once you are home.

Often for
Anyone recently discharged.
What to expect
A follow-up visit with your discharge summary.
