Questions

Common questions

Where does Dr Gangwal see patients?

At his own clinic, Paras Gangwal Clinic, in the DDA Market, Mansarovar Park, and in the OPD of East Delhi Medical Centre on Main G.T. Road, Shahdara. Online consultations are also possible.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly. If another doctor has referred you, bring their note and your reports.

What should I bring to my first visit?

All your recent reports and discharge summaries, your current medicines or their strips, and a list of your questions. For diabetes, bring your sugar readings if you test at home.

What is the fee?

The clinic tells you the fee when you book.

Which languages does he speak?

English and Hindi.

What if I need to be admitted?

If your illness needs hospital care, Dr Gangwal will tell you so clearly and guide you on admission. East Delhi Medical Centre, where he has been a consultant since 2001, is on Main G.T. Road in Shahdara.

Not feeling right? Start with a proper conversation.

Book a visit at the clinic or at East Delhi Medical Centre, or consult online. Bring your reports and medicines — Dr Gangwal will go through them with you, in English or Hindi.
