A physician for East Delhi

Trained at Maulana Azad Medical College, and a consultant physician at East Delhi Medical Centre since 2001.

Dr Paras Gangwal is a physician in internal medicine and critical care. He looks after adults with everyday illness, long-term conditions such as diabetes and high blood pressure, and illness serious enough to need a hospital bed.

He studied medicine at Maulana Azad Medical College, University of Delhi, where he took his MBBS in 1995 and his MD in Medicine in 1999. He is registered with the Delhi Medical Council (No. 3023).

Since 2001 he has been a consultant physician at East Delhi Medical Centre on Main G.T. Road, Shahdara, working in internal medicine and critical care. His professional profile also lists him as a physician, internist and critical care consultant with Max Hospital, Patparganj, from 2008. He sees patients at his own clinic, Paras Gangwal Clinic, in the DDA Market, Mansarovar Park.

He is listed on the team of the Indian Diabetes and Cardio Metabolic Diseases Association (IDCM) (opens in a new tab) for internal medicine and critical care. He consults in English and Hindi.
